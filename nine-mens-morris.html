Official Rules
Phase 1: Placement
Players alternate placing 9 pieces onto empty intersection points on the grid.

Phase 2: Movement
Once all 18 pieces are placed, players alternate sliding 1 piece per turn along connected grid lines to an adjacent empty node.

Mills & Captures
Forming 3 pieces in a connected straight line forms a Mill. This grants immediate removal of any opponent piece not currently inside an active mill (unless all enemy pieces are in mills).

Phase 3: Flying
When a player is reduced to exactly 3 pieces, their pieces can "fly" to any empty point on the board regardless of grid lines.

Victory Condition:
Reduce the opponent to fewer than 3 pieces, or block all of their legal moves.
