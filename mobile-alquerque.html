Official Rules (Libro de los Juegos)
Setup: Played on a 5x5 matrix with corner-to-corner diagonals and an inner diamond. White occupies top 10 nodes + left 2 on middle row. Black occupies bottom 10 nodes + right 2 on middle row. Center node starts empty.

Movement: Slide 1 piece per turn to an adjacent connected empty node.

Captures: Jump over an adjacent enemy piece along a connected line onto an empty space beyond. Captured piece is removed.

Mandatory Jumps & Multi-Jumps: Capturing is mandatory! If a jump exists, you must take it. If a piece lands with another valid jump available, it must continue jumping.

Victory Condition: Capture all enemy pieces or block opponent from having legal moves.
