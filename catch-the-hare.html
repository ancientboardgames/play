Official Rules (Libro de los Juegos)
Board Geometry: Traditional 5x5 Alquerque matrix with corner-to-corner main diagonals and an inner midpoint diamond.

Setup: 12 Hounds occupy Rows 0 & 1, plus the outer edges of Row 2. The Hare starts in the exact center.

Hare Victory: Reduce the Hounds to 9 or fewer pieces (capturing at least 3 Hounds). At 9 or fewer Hounds, trapping the Hare is mathematically impossible.

Hounds Victory: Block all valid moves and jumps of the Hare to trap and immobilize it.

Movement: Pieces move along grid connections. The Hare moves 1 step or jumps over adjacent Hounds to empty nodes in a straight line.

Controls: When playing as the Hare, click any valid destination node to move instantly without selecting the piece first.
