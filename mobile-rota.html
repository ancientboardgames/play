Official Rules
Board: A wheel with 8 outer nodes along the circumference and 1 center hub node.

Phase 1 (Placement): Players alternate placing 3 pieces each on empty nodes. You cannot place directly into the center hub to form an immediate 3-in-a-row win on turn 1.

Phase 2 (Movement): Players alternate sliding 1 piece along lines to an adjacent connected empty node (along spokes or round the rim).

Victory Condition: Form a straight line of 3 pieces across the center (a diameter) or around 3 consecutive outer rim nodes.
