Official Rules
Mill & Capture: Aligning 3 pieces in a row forms a mill. When you form a mill, targetable enemy pieces will glow red—tap one to capture it!

Phases: Place 9 pieces, slide along connected lines, and fly when reduced to 3 pieces.
