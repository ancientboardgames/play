Official Rules
Setup: Played on an 8×8 grid. Each player starts with 12 soldiers in their first two rows (full back row + middle four squares of second row).

Movement: Pieces move like a chess rook—any number of empty squares orthogonally. No jumping over other pieces!

Custodian Capture: Trap an enemy piece strictly between two enemy-flanking pieces (Enemy—Victim—Enemy). Adjacent friendly pieces protect each other.
Turn: White
Ludus Latrunculorum Help File

1. Legal Movement (Rook Slide & No Jumping)
Pieces slide any number of empty squares horizontally or vertically. You cannot jump over other pieces—friendly or enemy pieces block your path entirely.
Diagram: The White piece can slide freely up, down, or left along open squares. It cannot slide right past the Black piece because jumping or passing through pieces is illegal.
2. Custodian Capture (Strict Sandwiching)
An enemy piece is captured when it is bracketed on opposite flanks by enemy pieces (Enemy—Victim—Enemy). Pairs of adjacent friendly pieces mutually protect each other.
Before Move: White slides down into position.

Result: The single Black piece is flanked on opposite sides by White pieces and is captured!
3. Safe Entry Exception
If you voluntarily move your own piece into a gap between two enemy pieces, your piece is SAFE and is not captured.
Diagram: The White piece starts to the left and slides sideways into the gap between the two Black soldiers. Because White initiated the move into the trap voluntarily, White is completely safe.
4. Mutual Protection of Friendly Pairs
If two friendly pieces sit directly next to each other, they protect one another. Bracketing one side with a hostile piece does not capture them because one flank is friendly.
Diagram: Two adjacent Black pieces (`B B`) sit between a White piece on the left and a White piece on the right (`W B B W`). Neither Black piece is captured because each touches a friendly Black neighbor on one flank.
