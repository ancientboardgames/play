Welcome
Welcome to the retro arcade repository featuring accurate digital reconstructions of classic strategic board games. Featuring single-file implementations complete with Web Audio API sound synthesizers, local 2-player modes, and smart AI opponents. Choose a game below to begin play:
Game Library
Alquerque 1283 AD
The ancient predecessor to checkers. Features a 5x5 grid with diagonal lines, mandatory jumping captures, and multi-jump chains.
Launch Game >
Catch the Hare Cercar la Liebre
An asymmetric hunt game from King Alfonso X's Libro de los Juegos. Play as the nimble Hare or coordinate 12 Hounds to trap it.
Launch Game >
Ludus Latrunculorum Roman Mercenaries
Tactical Roman war game played on an 8x8 grid. Utilizes rook-like sliding movement, custodian sandwich captures, and mutual protection rules.
Launch Game >
Nine Men's Morris Classic Strategy
Classic three-phase positioning strategy. Form 3-in-a-row "mills" to remove opponent pieces, down to an endgame flying phase.
Launch Game >
Rota Roman Wheel Game
A swift Roman alignment game played on a wheel board with 8 rim nodes and a center hub. Place 3 pieces and slide along spokes to align 3-in-a-row.
